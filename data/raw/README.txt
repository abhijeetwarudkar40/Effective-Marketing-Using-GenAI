PersonalizedMarketingAI - Realistic Synthetic Banking Dataset
===============================================================

Synthetic dataset for the PersonalizedMarketingAI project.

Population
----------
600 synthetic banking customers.

Files
-----
customers.csv
transactions.csv
product_holdings.csv
products.csv
campaigns.csv
campaign_interactions.csv
recommendations.csv

Design goals
------------
- Internally consistent customer/product/campaign relationships.
- Meaningful transactional behavior for segmentation.
- Multiple levels of customer value.
- Active, at-risk, and dormant recency patterns.
- Variation in digital adoption and engagement.
- Product penetration suitable for cross-sell analysis.
- Campaign response and recommendation outcomes suitable for
  personalization and A/B testing demonstrations.

Important
---------
This data is entirely synthetic and must not be represented as real
customer data.

The latent generator profiles used to create behavioral variation are
NOT stored in customers.csv and must not be used as model features.
